title: TypeR 0.6 beta
date: 2024-06-25
version: 0.6.0
summary: Line spacing that follows the text size, five more languages and new size shortcuts.
---

- **Line spacing.** When you increase or decrease the size of the text with TypeR, the line spacing follows.
- **Multilanguage.** Spanish, German, Vietnamese, Ukrainian and Russian.
- **New keyboard shortcuts** to increase and decrease the size of the text.

## In short

- There is no account, no advertising and no tracking across apps or sites. We sell nothing about you, to anyone.
- Your documents stay on your device. A page only leaves it when you use a feature that needs an online service, and then it goes to that service alone.
- TypeR Studio sends anonymous usage statistics once a day. You can turn them off in **Settings → Anonymous usage statistics**.

This policy covers the TypeR panel for Photoshop, TypeR Studio (the web version at [typer.hayasaku.fr/web/](https://typer.hayasaku.fr/web/), the desktop app for macOS and Windows, and the app for iPhone, iPad and Android), and this site. They are made by Team ScanR, a team of volunteers, called "we" below.

## This site

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts, images and videos are served from the site itself, not from a third party.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in its logs to run and secure its service. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## What stays on your device

TypeR Studio keeps your settings, styles, swatches, shortcuts, recent files, the document you were working on, and any API key you enter, on your device: in your browser's storage for the web version, in the app's storage otherwise. The TypeR panel keeps its settings in Photoshop. None of this is sent to us.

To erase it, clear the site data of typer.hayasaku.fr in your browser, or uninstall the app.

On iPhone, iPad and Android, the app only opens the files, folders and photos you pick. It uses the camera, or the microphone for a video, only when you choose to take a photo or record a video to open it, and keeps nothing of either beyond the document you open.

## Anonymous usage statistics

To know how many people use TypeR Studio and what to improve, the app sends, at most once a day:

- a random number drawn on your device the first time, so that someone who opens the app ten times counts once;
- whether it is the web version or the desktop app, the operating system, whether the screen is a touch screen, the app's version and the interface language;
- for the days before, how many times each of TypeR Studio's tools and commands was used, and from where (shortcut, menu, toolbar…);
- the errors the app ran into, without what they were about.

The country is read from your IP address by our host, Cloudflare. The address itself is not stored. Nothing about you, your documents or what is in them is sent.

They go to `stats.teamscanr.fr`, a Cloudflare service run by us, and are kept there as daily totals per random number, only to count users and decide what to work on.

Nothing is sent from a browser that asks sites not to share (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track), nor from a browser driven by a script. **Settings → Anonymous usage statistics** turns them off, and forgets the random number and everything not yet sent.

## Feedback

When you send feedback (**Help → Send Feedback…**, or when the app asks what you think of it), we receive your rating, your comment if you write one, the app (web, desktop or mobile), the interface language and how many days you have used it. It is posted in a private channel of our Discord server, read by the team.

Your IP address is used for a minute to stop floods of messages, then forgotten. Don't put personal information in your comment unless you want us to read it.

## Features that send data to other services

These only send something when you use them. Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

| Feature | What is sent | To whom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generative Fill, with your Google key | The selection and what surrounds it, and your prompt | Google (Gemini API) |
| Generative Fill, with your fal.ai key | The selection and what surrounds it, and your prompt | fal.ai |
| Generative Fill, with your Adobe account (desktop app) | The selection and what surrounds it, and your prompt | Adobe (Firefly) |
| Detect Text and Remove Text | The page, as an image, and an anonymous token drawn for your installation | Temizle, by Safi Çizgi |
| Font FindeR | The names of the fonts searched for, and the fonts downloaded | Font FindeR (tools.carbonscans.com) |

Your API keys go only to the service they belong to, never to us. Generative Fill with LaMa Manga, or with FLUX in the desktop app, and Content-Aware Fill run on your device: nothing leaves it. Their models are downloaded once, from Hugging Face and GitHub, and the in-browser engine from jsDelivr. These services see your IP address like any download.

The desktop app and the TypeR panel also check for updates and announcements on this site and on GitHub. **Settings → Discord activity** in the desktop app shows your Discord friends that you are using TypeR Studio: it speaks to the Discord app on your computer, not to the network.

Links to Discord, GitHub or Ko-fi take you to those services, under their own policies.

## Children

TypeR and TypeR Studio are not aimed at children under 15, and we don't knowingly collect anything about them.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or erase data about you, or object to its use. Since the statistics carry only a random number and the feedback carries no name, we usually have no way of telling which data is yours: turning off the statistics and clearing the app's data are the surest way to erase it. For the services listed above, ask them directly.

If you think your data is mishandled, you can complain to the French data protection authority, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/en).
